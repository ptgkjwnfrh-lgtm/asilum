# Designer canon (working set)

The houses the archive currently reasons over most; affinity bridges use
their dominant tag signatures, not name strings.

Archive-minimal core: Helmut Lang (98–99 trompe l'oeil, Astro), Jil
Sander (late-90s tailoring), Maison Margiela (Tabi, 0-line artisanal).
Japanese vanguard: Yohji Yamamoto, Comme des Garçons (SS97 Body Meets
Dress), Junya Watanabe (AD2004 tech), Issey Miyake (heat-set pleats),
Undercover (Scab SS03), Number (N)ine (SS03 grunge).
Antwerp/poet: Ann Demeulemeester, Dries Van Noten.
Object/process: Carol Christian Poell (object-dye, drip rubber).
Grail-era references: Raf Simons AW01 Riot, Prada Sport 1999.

Add designers by tagging pieces, not by editing this list — the list
documents, the tags decide.
