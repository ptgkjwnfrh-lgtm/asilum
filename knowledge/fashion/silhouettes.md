# Silhouette & material axes

Silhouette axes the brain scores: VOLUME (low↔high), DRAPE, STRUCTURE,
RAW/FINISHED, MINIMAL/MAXIMAL, TECH/NATURAL. Fit data (sizing.js) feeds
fitScore for stylist + search "FITS ME".
Material vocabulary lives in the lexicon (garment tokens): denim, virgin
wool, mohair, horse leather, nylon, viscose, poly-pleat…
Eras matter: pieces carry season references (98AW, SS03) — era is a taste
signal, not trivia.
