## 2. What shipped

  real device counts (pins, names, houses followed, cities bought from), a

  7px rule under the masthead (`.gxthick`), numbered kickers (big faint
  numeral + Michroma kicker + note), the cover's `.cclbl/.ccval` credit stack
  in the rail (RECORD No. / PIECES PLACED / CONVICTIONS HELD), gutter

* **The map settles** at 0.4 after one painted frame at the warp's 0.5, so the
  passport→upload hand-off stays pixel-continuous (`.gxmap.settled`).

### THE PURCHASE GLOBE — `lib/globe.js` + `app/components/PurchaseGlobe.jsx`

* A wireframe earth drawn entirely from characters: latitude rings every
  22.5° and twelve meridians as **chains of `- / | \` glyphs** whose glyph
  follows the line's slope (`CHAIN` 94 per equator so the characters touch),
  the far hemisphere dimmed to a see-through cage, a dotted silhouette, a
  faint polar axis, the Codec's centre reticle, the dock's hologram
  scanlines. Drag to spin with inertia, arrow keys turn it, paused off-screen,
  one still frame under reduced motion, sized by `--ed-gx-globe` (250px, the
  bitmap follows at 2×). **Pure canvas, no React** — a harness page mounted
  it with real cities to prove the marker path (memory mode has no
  purchases).
* **Markers are the record, nothing else.** `GET /api/orders?places=1` reads
  the caller's PAID sale orders and purchase tickets reported
  bought / kept / returned, resolves each piece's house through
  `lib/asterisk/houses.js` and its city through the new curated
  `lib/asterisk/places.js` (one coordinate row per city the origin record
  names, provenance-stamped, `placeCoverage()` reports a hole), and answers
  `{ places, purchases, placed, unplaced, sources }`. A ticket-fee order is
  not counted (the piece behind it is the ticket). Bag intent never counts. A
  house without a city (Carhartt WIP, Cav Empt) is listed as **not placed**,
  never guessed. Nothing bought → `NO FIX — NOTHING BOUGHT YET`, the globe
  still turns. The readout prints the front-most marker's fix in D.MM.SS.
* **Performance (late in the day):** the cage's per-glyph `shadowBlur` cost
  /upload **37ms a frame at idle** in Chrome (16.7 on every other page). The
  cage draws with alpha alone now, at half rate (a drag draws every frame):
  /upload idles at 17.9 and scrolls at 18.9 (was 33).
* Tests: `tests/purchase-places.test.js` — coverage of houses ↔ places,
  coordinate ranges and country agreement, `placeFor` never guessing,
  `aggregatePlaces` arithmetic, the route in memory mode (a paid sale
  counts; an awaiting order and a fee-only order do not; another device sees
  nothing).

### ICE LIGHT — the cyan glass world (`[data-theme="light"]` in `app/globals.css`)

Three moves in one day, each on the owner's word: (1) sky-teal hue; (2)
softened — saturation down, not lightness up; (3) **the cyan glass world**:
a cool cyan-white ground (`--bg` #e6f4fa), cyan glass tints on the header,
pills, panes and their shadows, cyan lamps, a brighter cyan glow on every lit
word (rgba 0,190,235), chrome sheens kept white, the ink and signal at the
passing cyans (**#0c7795 / #0d779e**), the secondary accent a soft **purple
#805ac1**, the wash two aqua hazes plus a faint pink and a faint purple, the
blobs aqua / pink / purple / aqua, the vignette cyan. **The AA law is kept
throughout** (`tests/theme-contrast.test.js`): at any sky hue the lightest
passing value sits near lightness 0.32, so a pastel sky cannot pass — told to
the owner twice; relaxing the law is theirs to rule. **A selected, filled or
hovered control on ice is FROSTED** (pale sky fill, blue text) — the grouped
rule at the end of globals.css over every `background: var(--ink)` state —
never a dark block.

### THE HEADER'S WORDS ARE JUST WORDS — both themes

From the owner's Safari recording (a hovered word grew a dark grey pill in
ice): no pill on a hovered or current header word, `.glass-light` hidden
(the lamps), no pointer shine or rim catch on the pane, no meniscus, in
**both** themes. The current destination keeps its red LED and red bar. On
phosphor a word's resting light (the teal law's smear, now the token
`--word-smear`) stays and a hover changes nothing; on ice the header's words
carry no glow at all. The dark-pill mechanism in WebKit was **not
diagnosed** (no WebKit here); the effects were removed outright.

### THE PLUS FIELD — `.os-plus` in `app/shell.js`

A fixed layer carrying a holographic sweep in the theme's own hues (`--holo`:
dark = green / blue / purple / red / fuchsia; ice = cyan / aqua / pink /
purple / aqua) seen only through a mask of small pluses (`--plus`, an 8–11px
plus at 96 / 137px tilings, offset so it reads scattered). Opacity through
the design console (`--ed-plus-op`, shipped 0.05); **phosphor takes 75% of
the knob** (`--plus-k` 0.75 → 0.0375), ice takes it whole. Static.

### Safari — what it can show, and the bend's reach (#456)

WebKit never runs an SVG filter as a backdrop-filter (trap 150), so on Safari
the header's bezel **cannot** refract; the only warp is the **page-side
bend** (`createPageBend`), where content passes under the strip's bottom
edge while scrolling. Nothing on the new pages was in its selector — so
Safari showed no warp there. The selector in `app/shell.js` now reaches
`.headsub, .gxmastblock, .gxthick, .gxhead, .gxfoot, .rkhead, .rkrow, .pfsec,
.otfrow, .ppsec, .demobanner`. Two bounds joined `LiquidGlass.jsx`: an
element over **260k px²** (`BIG_AREA`) passes unbent (WebKit re-rasterises
the whole element through three displacement passes per placement), and
placement runs on **alternate scroll frames**. Proven on Chrome's lab switch
(`?lgpage=1`), which forces the page-side path: the upload masthead, the rule
and the section heads bend with the fringe; 16.7ms a frame on /settings.

## 6. Traps learned today (153–159)

* **153** — `preview_start` reads `~/.claude/launch.json`, not the repo's; a
  stale entry there pointed at a removed worktree and the tool cd'd into
  nothing.
* **154** — a hidden Browser pane cannot composite: screenshot and scroll time
  out. Headless Chrome over CDP is the substitute; the CDP result shape is
  `r.result.result.value` and an async expression needs `awaitPromise`.
* **155** — `tests/a11y-focus-visible.test.js` recognises only `:focus`
  (":focus-visible" minus ":focus" is "-visible", not a prefix). Never write
  `outline: none`; style `:focus-visible` with an outline instead.
* **156** — `npm test` regenerates `scripts/.vibe-sweep.json` (580 lines,
  re-sorted) and it is tracked: check `git status` before `git add -A`.
* **157** — a macOS screen recording's filename carries a NARROW NO-BREAK
  SPACE before AM/PM: copy it with a glob. The Bash sandbox cannot read
  `~/Desktop`; no ffmpeg here — frames via a Swift `AVAssetImageGenerator`
  script, loading `duration` with `loadValuesAsynchronously` first.
* **158** — a canvas `shadowBlur` per glyph, ~700 glyphs, sixty times a
  second, cost a page 37ms a frame at idle. Draw glow once or not at all.
* **159** — a page's SCROLL cost can be its IDLE cost. Measure idle first,
  page by page, before blaming the scroll path (the bend was innocent).

   real Chrome with the owner's hardware is the honest number.
4. **`aggregatePlaces` and `houseOrigin` spelling**: the aggregate keys by the
   origin record's brand spelling; a catalog brand spelled differently from
   `HOUSES` (accents, case) resolves through `houseOrigin`'s normalisation —
   check a brand with a diacritic end to end.

   760px; `/u/[handle]` and `/piece/[id]` keep the enlarged `.headline` with
   no second line — a long handle at 84px was not looked at.
