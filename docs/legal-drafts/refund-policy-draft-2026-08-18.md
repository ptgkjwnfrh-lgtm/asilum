## Proposed terms (plain language, ASILUM voice to be applied later)

1. **Who you buy from.** Purchases on *ASILUM magazine are fulfilled and
   shipped by the independent designer named on the piece. ASILUM operates
   the catalog, checkout, and support channel; the designer makes and ships
   the garment. [Counsel: confirm this framing keeps merchant-of-record
   obligations where we intend — see open question 1.]

2. **Cancellation window.** A paid order may be cancelled for a full refund
   any time before the designer marks it shipped.

3. **Returns.** Unworn pieces with tags attached may be returned within
   **14 days of delivery** for a refund of the item price. Return shipping
   is paid by the buyer unless the piece arrived damaged, misdescribed, or
   wrong. [Counsel: 14 days vs. state/provincial minimums; Quebec and
   California consumer rules.]

4. **Refund method and timing.** Refunds go to the original payment method
   via Stripe within 5 business days of the return arriving with the
   designer. Payment processing fees are not deducted from the buyer's
   refund.

5. **Final-sale items.** Made-to-order and made-to-measure pieces are
   final sale unless damaged or misdescribed, and are labeled as such
   before checkout. [Counsel: enforceability by jurisdiction.]

6. **Damaged or misdescribed.** Photos within 7 days of delivery; ASILUM
   arranges return shipping at no cost and refunds in full, or replaces
   where the designer can.

7. **Disputes.** Contact ASILUM first (support email); chargebacks filed
   without contacting support may slow resolution but never remove the
   buyer's statutory rights.

## Open questions for counsel

1. Merchant of record: with Stripe (not Connect) and ASILUM taking payment
   then remitting to designers, is ASILUM the seller of record in US/CA?
   Does that change with Stripe Connect (planned when designer count grows)?
2. Sales-tax collection duties per state/province at ten-orders scale
   (marketplace-facilitator thresholds are far away, but confirm floor).
3. Required French-language consumer text if Quebec buyers are in scope.
4. Whether the demo-catalog disclosure needs mirroring in the terms while
   demo and real items coexist.

## Explicitly out of scope (v1, per campaign §3)

(14-day withdrawal directive) — none apply to a domestic designer-ships v1
and writing them now would imply features that do not exist.
