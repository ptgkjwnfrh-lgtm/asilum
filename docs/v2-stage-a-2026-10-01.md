## Proposed defaults (the brief asks that they be reported)

- Ad strip rotation: 7 s per placement, no rotation under reduced motion,
  paused on hover, focus and while typing. Not announced to screen readers.
- Sheet initial height 60 vh; `EXPAND` and any focused field inside lift it
  to the full height.
- Search sheet: 12 pieces, 8 Wire posts per answer; "Search all ASiLUM"
  leads to `/discover?q=` for the full page.
- ASTERISK on → `/api/discover?brain=1` (the Passport-guided order);
  off → plain relevance.

## Web deep links (a deliberate reading of "fresh launch = The Feed")

A browser opens the URL it is given. The app never restores a last-visited
tab on its own (there is no such memory; the test pins it), and a PWA
launch opens `start_url` = `/`. A link to `/cover` or `/map` opened from
outside IS a deep link in the brief's sense: the Feed is the base
destination beneath it and the dock's THE FEED is one tap away. Forcing a
redirect would break every shared link to the cover and the map.

## Known gaps after this stage

- The feed does not yet save/restore its anchor through `lib/viewstate.js`
  ("New pieces" button, anchor restore after a detail → DM → back round
  trip). The registry and its pure parts are tested; the wiring is next.
- The neighbourhood names arrive in the road document but `StreetMap.jsx`
  does not draw them yet.
- The Studio's promotion request could never list a draft: the filter looked
  for `kind === "place"` while the draft's `kind` is its venue kind. Fixed
  here (`d.kind !== "promotion"`).
- `docs/CODE-MAP.md` must be regenerated (`npm run docs:codemap`) before
  merge.
